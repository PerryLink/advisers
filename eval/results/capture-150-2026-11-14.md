## Re-run after deterministic date pass (2026-11-14, `advisers@0.2.0-preview.57-dev`)

Model: `deepseek-flash` (unchanged). Same 5×30 synthetic generator, but the
script now reports per-case failures, counts rule-resolved dates, and gates
`dueRate >= 95%` (non-zero exit otherwise). Label convention v2: a `周X` said on
that same weekday resolves to today (natural); the 9 such cases keep a `dueAlt`

silently dropped.

Why the legacy number is 94.0%: the only 9 legacy mismatches are the 9
same-weekday ambiguity labels (e.g. "周五前" sent on a Friday). The rule pass
takes the natural reading (today), the legacy label expects next week. This is a
label-convention difference, not a model/date error; both readings are reported
so the old 84.7% is not silently rewritten.

Before this change the same set scored 127/150 (84.7%) with no per-case
breakdown. The improvement is the deterministic `relative-date.js` pass plus a
prompt that gives the model the Asia/Shanghai wall clock (the old prompt showed
a UTC timestamp, e.g. 00:30 Beijing displayed as the previous UTC day).

Caveat unchanged: synthetic generator, not the human gold set; do not use as an
external benchmark claim.
