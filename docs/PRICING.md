## 1. Who needs a commercial licence

Advisers is **source-available** software under the PolyForm Noncommercial
License 1.0.0 (SPDX: `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`); documentation, data and
design are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Noncommercial use is permitted by the public licence.
Any other use — including a for-profit organisation internal trial or evaluation,
paid client work by an individual professional, or building the software into
something sold — is **commercial use** and requires a separate written commercial
licence; until that agreement is signed, commercial use is not licensed. The
authoritative wording is [TOS.md](TOS.md) section 3.1.1/3.1.2 and
[LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) (“Commercial use in client work”); this page only
summarises it. If in doubt, ask first through the channel in
[COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) section 4.

Noncommercial use stays under the public PolyForm-NC licence. It is not a “free
tier” of a commercial product and it gets no separate commercial feature limits.
Permitted purposes and their exact definitions are in
[LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) (“Permitted purposes”) and [TOS.md](TOS.md)

## 4. 30-day evaluation

Evaluation is a **legal mechanism, not a marketing trial**. It cannot open before
a lawyer-approved evaluation licence text exists (owner + lawyer; D1 item 2 in
[COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) section 3). Until then the only honest state is
“expression of interest, not available”. There is **no public trial** and no
runnable commercial evaluation build. Process summary, without terms:
[COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) section 5.

## 6. Support expectations

Advisers is local software: there is no online service and no availability
promise. [TOS.md](TOS.md) section 13.1 provides the software as-is, and this line
promises no response time or service-level support target. A future written
commercial licence may define support targets in its own text; this page does not.

## 7. Data and privacy

Local-first: no account, no telemetry, no hosted backend; the ledger and generated
files live on the user machine. Model calls remain an explicit network action
where configured. A portable bundle is **plaintext** and may contain client notes
and mail-account metadata, so it is not an encryption or access-control feature.
See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md), [TOS.md](TOS.md) section 3.1 and
[PORTABILITY.md](PORTABILITY.md) sections 7/11.
