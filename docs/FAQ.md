# Commercial-licensing FAQ

> Twelve answers to the questions that come up before a commercial licence can be
> bought. D1 (2026-11-29) and D8 (2026-12-06) have not decided prices, the selling
> entity, invoicing, evaluation terms or refunds, so several answers stop at
> “pending”. The authoritative legal text is [TOS.md](TOS.md) and
> [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md); this FAQ only points at it and is not legal
> advice. Process and decision package: [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md). Price page
> skeleton: [PRICING.md](PRICING.md). Last reviewed 2026-11-24.

## 1. What counts as commercial use?

**Short answer.** Paid client work, use inside a for-profit organisation (including
an internal trial or evaluation), building the software into something you sell, or
any use from which you reasonably expect commercial application or consideration.
The exact definitions are [TOS.md](TOS.md) section 3.1.1 and
[LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) (“Commercial use in client work”).

**Status.** Settled by the current TOS/LICENSING text. If it is unclear, ask first
through [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) section 3.

## 2. Is noncommercial use free?

**Short answer.** Yes — the public PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 licence permits
noncommercial purposes, including personal use without anticipated commercial
application and use by charitable, educational, public research, public safety or
health, environmental and government institutions. It is not a “free tier” of a
commercial product and there is no separate commercial feature list.

**Status.** Settled by [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) and [TOS.md](TOS.md)
section 3.1.1.

## 3. Is Advisers released under an OSI-approved licence?

**Short answer.** No. The software is `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`, a
source-available licence; documentation, data and design are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The
repository is source-available, not an OSI-approved project, and the docs say so
([NOTICE](../NOTICE), [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md)).

**Status.** Settled by the current licence files.

## 4. Can I use Advisers for a paid client deliverable?

**Short answer.** Only after a separate written commercial licence is signed.
Using the software to produce work for a paying client, or embedding its output in
a paid deliverable, is commercial use and is not licensed before that signature
([TOS.md](TOS.md) section 3.1.1, [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md)).

**Status.** Settled as a requirement; the licence text itself is pending D1 item 2/4
and the lawyer. No commercial licence can be issued yet.

## 5. Does a for-profit organisation internal trial count as commercial use?

**Short answer.** Yes. The public licence does not cover a for-profit organisation
internal trial, evaluation or test, so even an evaluation needs a written
authorisation ([LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) “Commercial use in client work”;
[TOS.md](TOS.md) section 3.1.1(a)).

**Status.** Settled as a requirement; the 30-day evaluation mechanism is pending
D1 item 2 and is not open.

## 6. Can I try Advisers before buying?

**Short answer.** There is no public trial today and no runnable commercial
evaluation build. A 30-day evaluation is a proposed legal mechanism that can open
only after a lawyer-approved evaluation licence text exists; until then the honest
state is “expression of interest, not available”. The synthetic `advisers demo`
walkthrough and recordings are the only preview artifacts.

**Status.** Pending D1 item 2 + lawyer. See [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md)
section 5.

## 7. What does a commercial licence grant, and can I resell or embed it?

**Short answer.** Scope, term, fees, delivery, support and liability are defined
only by the written commercial licence agreement; this FAQ does not restate them.
Candidate tiers (when they exist) define the legal scope, not feature gates. The
public licence does not allow sublicensing or transfer, and the Advisers/PerryLink
names and logos are not licensed ([LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md)). Reselling,
embedding the software in a product, or redistributing a derivative requires the
written agreement and, for derivative distributions, the conditions in
[DERIVED-PROJECTS.md](DERIVED-PROJECTS.md).

**Status.** Pending D1 items 4 (contributor rights/trademark) and the lawyer-drafted
licence; no grant exists today.

## 8. Do I need an invoice or a receipt?

**Short answer.** Decide what your organisation needs before asking; invoicing
depends on the selling entity, which D1 has not fixed. There is no invoice
promise today, and no payment should be sent before the written licence and
payment details exist. Receipts, ordinary VAT invoices and special VAT invoices
are only possible if the chosen entity can issue them.

**Status.** Pending D1 item 1 (selling entity and invoicing).

## 9. Is the 30-day evaluation itself commercial use?

**Short answer.** For a for-profit organisation, yes: evaluation and internal
trials are commercial use under the current licence, so they require an explicit
written evaluation licence even when no money is paid. Noncommercial evaluation by
a permitted noncommercial user stays under the public licence.

**Status.** Settled as a requirement; the evaluation mechanism is pending D1
item 2 and is not open.

## 10. Are non-profits, students and public institutions covered?

**Short answer.** Charitable organisations, educational institutions, public
research organisations, public safety or health organisations, environmental
protection organisations and government institutions are permitted noncommercial
users under the public licence; individual students doing private study are also
covered when there is no anticipated commercial application. There is no separate
“free commercial licence” for these groups.

**Status.** Settled by [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md) (“Permitted purposes”) and
[TOS.md](TOS.md) section 3.1.1.

## 11. How do I ask a licensing question without posting personal data?

**Short answer.** Open a GitHub issue with the title prefix `[commercial-license]`
and describe only the request type (evaluation, individual, studio, other) and the
use case in general terms. Do not post a legal entity name, contact details, tax
ID, client names, contracts, credentials or ledger data: public issues are stored
by GitHub and may be outside your jurisdiction ([TOS.md](TOS.md) section 15.3).
Details will be requested through the private channel once D1 item 3 exists.

**Status.** Public triage exists; the private channel and its data fields are
pending D1 item 3 ([COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) section 3).

## 12. Why is there no public price?

**Short answer.** D1 has not decided the selling entity, the evaluation text or
the invoicing capability, so a public number could not be honoured. [PRICING.md](PRICING.md)
is the single future price source and currently marks every row “pending / 待开放”.
Do not treat any number in older research notes or third-party summaries as a
current price or offer.

**Status.** Pending D1 (2026-11-29); numbers will appear only in
[PRICING.md](PRICING.md) after the decision.
