# Pricing / channel / trust assets (E11, research recommendation)

> Not a commitment. Based on the 2026-11-14 competitive/verification research.

## Price positioning

- MIT core stays free; no per-seat/enterprise pricing for the target user.
- Optional local pro/support subscription at a personal price point (roughly
  $10–40/user/month equivalent); do not gate core evidence/audit features.
- Do not price against enterprise CLM (¥2.4万–28万 projects) or per-seat suites;
  those buyers are not the target.

## Channel

- Independent-consultant communities and open-source distribution; DSH/agent
  ecosystem; no enterprise procurement/招投标.
- Customer-visible artifacts: `.ics` reminders, `deliveries --pack` evidence
  bundles, `deliver --format pdf/xlsx` deliverables.
- Trust assets: data-flow diagram, `advisers privacy --prove`, independent
  human-gold report (pending), real dogfood report (pending), reproducible eval
  scripts/results.

## Verified competitive anchors (2026-11-14)

- Fyxer: Pro $37–49 / Team $64–99, 14-day trial (no free tier).
- Gemini/Workspace: $7–22; Copilot $30 add-on; Todoist/滴答/Motion/飞书 AI
  subscriptions; Chinese suites ¥19–¥398/month plus enterprise private quotes.
- Advisers: MIT core free; any paid tier must be optional and local-first.

## Do not claim

"Free unlimited everything", "enterprise compliance", "secure/encrypted" until
the corresponding gates/evidence exist; "offline" is also inaccurate while model
calls use a cloud provider.
